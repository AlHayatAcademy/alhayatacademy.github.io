Syllabi were published for several **Junior Clerk (BS-11)** advertisements in the Board of Revenue offices:

| Case | Office | Posts |
|---|---|---|
| 28J2026 | Bahawalpur Division | 8 |
| 30J2026 | Deputy Commissioner, Kot Addu | 10 |
| 32J2026 | Additional Deputy Commissioner (Revenue), Kot Addu | 2 |

All use one MCQ paper of 100 marks and 90 minutes: **about 80% General Ability** and **about 20% MS Office and computer skills**. Only candidates who qualify the General Ability test are called for the typing and proficiency test.
