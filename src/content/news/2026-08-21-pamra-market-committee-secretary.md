PPSC published the syllabus for **Secretary Market Committee (BS-16)** in the Punjab Agricultural Marketing Regulatory Authority: 9 posts for A-class committees (Case 47J2026) and 15 for B-class committees (Case 46J2026), each on a 5-year contract.

**Paper split:** 60% qualification related questions and 40% General Ability (General Knowledge, Pakistan Studies, Current Affairs, Geography, Everyday Science, Basic Mathematics, English, Urdu and Basic Computer Studies).

Practise the General Ability part on our [Market Committee Secretary page](/posts/market-committee-secretary/).
