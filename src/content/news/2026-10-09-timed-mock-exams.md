We added **timed mock exams** for Junior Clerk, Assistant, Deputy Accountant, Junior Computer Operator and other posts. Each attempt draws a new paper from the question bank in the subject proportions printed in the syllabus.

After you submit you get your net score (correct minus 0.25 per wrong answer), a **subject-wise breakdown** to find weak areas, and a full review with explanations. Start from the [mock exam page](/exam/).
