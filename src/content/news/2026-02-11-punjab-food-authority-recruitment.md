PPSC published syllabi through 2026 for **13 post types** in the Punjab Food Authority (Price Control & Commodities Management Department), totalling **56 vacancies** on 3-year contracts:

Assistant Food Safety Officer (18), Veterinary Specialist (6), Assistant Public Analyst Category II (6), Dairy Technologist (5), Assistant Microbiologist (5), IT Administrator (4), Master Trainer (3), Assistant Public Analyst Category I (3), Senior Auditor (2), Food Safety Officer (1), Government Public Analyst (1), Assistant Director Budget & Accounts (1) and Public Relations Officer (1).

Most are **100% qualification related MCQs**. The Public Relations Officer paper is 60% media studies, 20% English and 20% Urdu. See the [syllabus table](/syllabus/) and the [PRO page](/posts/public-relations-officer/).
