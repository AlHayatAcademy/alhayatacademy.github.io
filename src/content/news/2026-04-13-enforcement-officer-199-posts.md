PPSC published the syllabus for **199 Enforcement Officer (BS-17)** posts in the Punjab Revenue Authority, Finance Department (Case No. 02-RA/2026, Advertisement No. 05/2026). The posts are on contract for 3 years, likely to be extended.

**Paper:** one MCQ paper, 100 marks, 90 minutes.

- **50%** Accounting, Finance and the Sales Tax Act, 2012 (Sales Tax on Services)
- **50%** General Ability: General Knowledge, Pakistan Studies, Current Affairs, Islamic Studies (GK for non-Muslims), Geography, Basic Mathematics, English, Urdu, Everyday Science and Basic Computer Studies

Prepare the General Ability half with our [Enforcement Officer page](/posts/enforcement-officer/) and the [General Ability master plan](/posts/general-ability/). Confirm dates and eligibility in the official advertisement.
