Syllabi appeared for the Deputy Commissioner / District Collector Office, Faisalabad (Board of Revenue):

- **Assistant (BS-16):** 5 posts, 3-year contract. One General Ability MCQ paper (100 marks, 90 minutes) including Basic Computer Studies.
- **Junior Clerk (BS-11):** 19 posts. 80% General Ability, 20% MS Office and computer skills, then a typing and proficiency test.
- **Stenographer (BS-15):** 17 posts. English shorthand at 70 wpm and typing at 35 wpm, then a computer proficiency test and Urdu shorthand and typing.
