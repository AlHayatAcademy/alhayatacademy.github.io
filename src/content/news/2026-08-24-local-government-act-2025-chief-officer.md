PPSC published the syllabus for **30 posts of Chief Officer / Municipal Officer (Regulations) (BS-16)**, reserved as service quota for Local Council Service employees, Punjab Local Government Board staff and Union Council Secretaries (BS-14).

The paper (100 marks, 90 minutes) has two parts: **the Punjab Local Government Act, 2025**, and **General Ability** (General Knowledge, Pakistan Studies, English, Urdu, Basic Mathematics and Basic Computer Studies). The split between the two is not printed in the syllabus.
