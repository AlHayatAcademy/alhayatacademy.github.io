The syllabus for **60 posts of Deputy Accountant (BS-16)** in the Punjab Treasuries & Accounts Service (Finance Department) is a single MCQ paper of 100 marks and 90 minutes. Four posts are reserved for special persons and three for minorities.

The paper is **General Ability** only: General Knowledge, Pakistan Studies, Current Affairs, Islamic Studies (GK for non-Muslims), Geography, Basic Mathematics, English, Urdu, Everyday Science and Basic Computer Studies.
