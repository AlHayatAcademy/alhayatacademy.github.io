Most PPSC recruitment papers are **one MCQ paper of 100 marks in 90 minutes**. That is less than a minute per question, so speed and recall matter more than deep essays. This plan works for General Ability posts such as Junior Clerk, Assistant and Deputy Accountant.

## Step 1: Read your syllabus first (Day 1)
Open your post on our [syllabus page](/posts/) and note the split. A Junior Clerk paper is about 80% General Ability and 20% MS Office; an Assistant paper is 100% General Ability. If your syllabus is 100% qualification related, this plan does not apply, because the paper tests your degree subject.

## Step 2: Measure your starting level (Day 1)
Take a [mock exam](/exam/) cold. Do not worry about the score. The subject-wise table at the end shows where you lose marks. Write the three weakest subjects on a sheet of paper.

## Step 3: Cover the subjects in weight order (Days 2 to 20)
Give extra days to subjects with more questions, and to your weak ones.

| Days | Focus |
|---|---|
| 2–5 | Pakistan Studies and Current Affairs |
| 6–8 | General Knowledge and Geography |
| 9–11 | English: vocabulary, grammar, voice, narration |
| 12–14 | Urdu: grammar, literature, proverbs |
| 15–16 | Basic Maths and reasoning |
| 17–18 | Everyday Science |
| 19–20 | Computer and MS Office |

For each subject: read the topic notes briefly, do the [practice test](/practice/), then read **every explanation**, including the ones you got right.

## Step 4: Revise with an error notebook (Days 21 to 25)
Keep a small notebook (or phone note) with every question you got wrong. Write the fact in one line. Review it each morning. Wrong answers are the fastest way to gain marks because they point to exactly what you do not know.

## Step 5: Full mock exams (Days 26 to 30)
Take one timed mock exam a day. Practise the exam routine:

1. First pass: answer what you know in about 50 minutes.
2. Second pass: return to the skipped ones and eliminate options.
3. Last 10 minutes: check the answers you marked as doubtful.

## How many hours?
Two to three focused hours a day beat six distracted ones. Spread them: one hour of new material, one of practice questions, and 30 minutes on your error notebook.

## Common mistakes
- Studying only your favourite subject.
- Reading explanations only for wrong answers.
- Skipping mock exams until the last week.
- Ignoring Urdu and computer questions, which are easy marks.
