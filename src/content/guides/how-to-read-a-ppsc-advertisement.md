A PPSC advertisement packs a lot into a few lines. Here is how to read the parts that matter for your preparation.

## Case number
Each recruitment has a **case number** such as *25J2025* or *02-RA/2026*. Use it to find the right syllabus and any later notices on the official website, and to avoid mixing up advertisements for the same post in different districts.

## Basis of appointment
- **Regular:** a permanent post.
- **Contract:** for a fixed period, often *3 years*, sometimes *5 years*, and in some cases "likely to be extended".

## Quotas
Ads often reserve some posts for women, minorities, special persons, or children of employees. This changes how many seats you compete for, not the paper.

## Where the posts are
Many Junior Clerk and Assistant posts are in specific district offices (Board of Revenue). Check the district named in your advertisement.

## The syllabus
The syllabus appears in a separate document linked to the case. It tells you:

1. **Paper type** (MCQ, number of marks and duration).
2. **Split** between General Ability and subject questions.
3. **Later stages** such as typing and proficiency tests or interviews.

Our [syllabus page](/syllabus/) summarises recent ones, but your own advertisement decides.

## Dates and fees
We do not copy closing dates or fees because they change. Take them from the official advertisement.
