Rules differ between tests, so treat this as a general checklist and follow the instructions on your roll number slip.

## The day before
- Find the exam centre and plan your travel so you arrive early.
- Prepare your **roll number slip** and **original CNIC**, plus anything else your slip asks for.
- Sleep early. A tired brain loses more marks than an unread chapter.
- Do only light revision of your error notebook.

## What to carry
- Roll number slip and original CNIC.
- Black pen (spare one) and a pencil if answer sheets are bubble-based.
- Items your slip says are required. Many centres do not allow mobile phones, calculators or bags; check the slip.

## At the centre
- Arrive well before the reporting time.
- Read the instructions on the paper fully: number of questions, marking, how to mark answers.
- Fill personal details exactly as required (roll number, paper code).

## Managing the 90 minutes
1. Spend the first 50 minutes on a first pass: answer what you know.
2. Use about 30 minutes on the skipped questions with elimination.
3. Keep the last 10 minutes to transfer or check answers and to make sure the sheet is correctly filled.

## Bubble sheet tips
- Fill the circle completely and darkly.
- Avoid stray marks. Do not erase repeatedly.
- Check the question number each time you mark.

## After the paper
Do not discuss answers in a way that rattles you. If there is a later stage (typing test, interview), start preparing for it straight away.

Practise the routine with a timed [mock exam](/exam/).
