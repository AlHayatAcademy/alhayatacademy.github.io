You have about **54 seconds per question**. These techniques help you finish with time to review.

## 1. Use a two-pass strategy
On the first pass answer only questions you can solve in under 30 seconds. Mark the others and move on. On the second pass spend the saved time on harder questions. Never let one question eat three minutes.

## 2. Eliminate before you choose
Cross out clearly wrong options first. Even removing one raises your chance from 25% to 33%.

## 3. Read the question stem fully
Words like **NOT**, **EXCEPT**, **first**, **largest** and **only** change the answer. Underline them mentally.

## 4. Watch for look-alike options
PPSC often places similar years, names or numbers together (1947, 1948, 1949). If you remember the fact with certainty, choose it. If not, think about the event around it.

## 5. Use "None of these" carefully
When "None of these" appears, check every option properly before choosing it.

## 6. Do calculations on paper, not in your head
Basic maths is short: percentage, ratio, average, simple interest. Write each step. Use shortcuts: 10% is dividing by 10; 25% is dividing by 4.

## 7. Learn grammar patterns, not rules
For English questions, test the sentence aloud: "She has gone" sounds right; "She have gone" does not. For voice and narration, learn the tense conversion table once.

## 8. Do not change an answer without a reason
Your first answer is more often right. Change it only if you remember a specific fact or spot a clear mistake.

## 9. Keep a steady rhythm
Check the clock every 20 questions: about 18 minutes per 20.

## Practise under pressure
Speed comes from practice. Take timed [mock exams](/exam/) and review the explanations.
