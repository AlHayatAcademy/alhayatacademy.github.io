Many PPSC syllabi simply say **General Ability** and list a group of subjects. This guide explains what that means in practice.

## The subject list
In the syllabi we reviewed, General Ability covers:

1. General Knowledge
2. Pakistan Studies
3. Current Affairs
4. Islamic Studies (General Knowledge for non-Muslim candidates)
5. Geography
6. Basic Mathematics
7. English
8. Urdu
9. Everyday Science
10. Basic Computer Studies

## How it is weighted
The syllabi do not print a percentage for each subject. They do print how General Ability fits into the whole paper:

| Post | General Ability share |
|---|---|
| Assistant, Deputy Accountant | 100% (includes Basic Computer Studies) |
| Junior Clerk | About 80%, plus about 20% MS Office and computer skills |
| Enforcement Officer, Deputy Director (Environment) | 50% |
| Secretary Market Committee | 40% |

Because no per-subject split is printed, the safe approach is to prepare every subject, with slightly more time on the broad ones: General Knowledge, Pakistan Studies and Current Affairs.

## What the questions look like
PPSC General Ability questions are mostly **direct recall**:

- Who, when, where: "The first Prime Minister of Pakistan was..."
- Superlatives: highest, longest, largest, first.
- Pairing: author and book, inventor and invention, organisation and headquarters.
- Language: antonyms, one-word substitutions, idioms, voice and narration, Urdu poets and proverbs.
- Short numerical problems: percentage, ratio, average, simple interest.
- Computer: shortcuts, file extensions, MS Office functions.

Options are four, often with look-alike distractors.
